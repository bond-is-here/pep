## Description

A little pep. A stronger you.

Pep is a cheerful place to keep your workouts. Build a routine you enjoy, start
in a tap, and record the work you put in. No account, ads, subscriptions, or
locked features. Just you, your next set, and a friendly little workout buddy.

MAKE IT YOUR WORKOUT

Start with an editable upper-body, lower-body, or full-body routine. Choose from
the exercise library or add your own moves. Arrange your exercises and set your
repetitions, working sets, and rest times.

KEEP YOUR FLOW

Log weights and reps, check off sets, and use the built-in rest timer. Add or
remove sets as you go. Resume an unfinished workout, and pick up with your
previously completed weights the next time you use that routine.

SEE YOUR LITTLE WINS

Look back through saved workouts, weekly activity, and training volume. Reach
workout milestones without a daily streak to protect. Today's routine suggestion
rotates from your log, and you can always choose a different one.

CHECK IN, IF YOU WANT TO

Body-weight tracking is optional. Save a check-in and see your trend over time.
Use kilograms or pounds for your workouts and check-ins.

PICK YOUR PEP

Choose Play, Poolside, or Punch for a fresh mix of colors. Enjoy a wiggly workout
buddy and little celebrations, or turn motion off. Pep also respects your
device's Reduce Motion setting.

KEEP YOUR LOG WITH YOU

Workout logging works offline, and your log stays on your device unless you
choose to export it. Save a backup through Files and restore it when you need
to. Pep has no automatic cloud sync or developer-operated workout server.

A little effort. A lot to feel good about. Always free.

## Reviewer notes

Pep is a free workout and routine tracker. No sign-in, demo credentials,
subscription, or in-app purchase is required. All app features are available
without a purchase. There are no advertisements or analytics SDKs.

The app opens with three editable starter routines and no workout or weight
history. To try the main flow:

1. On Today, select Start workout.
2. Enter a workout weight and repetitions, then check off a set. A load of zero
   can be used for a bodyweight set.
3. Skip or wait for the rest timer. Finish the workout and confirm saving the
   completed sets if the remaining sets are unfinished.
4. Open Progress to view the saved session and its completed-set volume.
5. Open Routines to create or edit a routine. Settings contains appearance,
   kilograms/pounds, weekly goal, and backup/restore controls.

Body-weight check-ins are optional and are not needed to use the workout
features. Pep does not access Apple Health or HealthKit.

Workouts, check-ins, and preferences are stored locally. Pep does not perform
automatic network requests or app cloud synchronization. Backup export and
restore use the system Files interface; any cloud storage is selected by the
user. Restoring requires confirmation and is unavailable while a workout is
active. A preserved recovery copy, when present, can be exported separately as
an unchanged file that may require repair.

Support uses public GitHub Issues. The support and privacy pages ask users not
to post personal workout, body-weight, or medical information there.
