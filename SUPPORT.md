# Pep support

Last updated: September 7, 2026

For app problems, questions, or feature requests, use [Pep GitHub Issues](https://github.com/bond-is-here/pep/issues).
Search existing issues first, then open a new issue if needed. Posting requires
a GitHub account; using Pep does not.

Issues are public. Do not include health or medical information, body
measurements, personal workout entries, exported files, passwords, or private
screenshots. Use fictional sample data when demonstrating a problem.

## Reporting a problem

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Pep version/build if known, the screen
or action involved, and what you expected versus what happened. Describe
reproducible steps using sample data. If Pep shows an error, include its wording
only after checking it for private information. The maintainers cannot access
your on-device routines or workout history.

## Getting started

Today suggests a routine based on your completed log, with a one-tap Start
workout action and a routine chooser. This rotation is a convenience, not a
recovery recommendation. Routines
contains three editable starter plans, a searchable exercise library, and a
custom exercise option. During a workout, edit the weight and repetitions in
each set row, then check off the set. Completing a set starts its rest timer.
You can add sets up to the per-exercise limit, or skip a timer.

Progress shows completed sessions, total volume, session details, and optional
body-weight check-ins. Weight tracking is optional and is never required to use
Pep. Settings changes the unit, weekly goal, palette, and motion preference.
Milestones celebrate saved workouts without a daily streak requirement.

## Backing up and restoring

Open Settings > Back up & restore > Save to Files. Keep the resulting backup
private; it includes your workout and weight history. Restore using Choose a
backup, review its counts, and confirm replacement. Export your current log
first if you want to retain it. Finish or discard an active workout before
restoring. Backups include the unit and weekly goal; colors and motion remain
device preferences. A cancelled or invalid restore leaves your current data
unchanged. Files over 20 MB are not accepted.

## Storage errors and starting over

If a change cannot be saved, check available iPhone storage and retry. Workout
edits stay open, and Pep won't finish the workout until saving succeeds. You
can also export a backup of your current progress. If an
existing workout archive cannot be opened, Pep preserves a recovery backup and
displays a storage error. Do not delete the app as a troubleshooting step if
you need to keep local history.

To intentionally erase all local Pep information, open Settings > General >
iPhone Storage > Pep > Delete App and confirm. Offloading keeps the data.
Backups and publicly posted support requests are separate; follow the
[privacy policy's retention and deletion instructions](PRIVACY.md).

## Price and privacy

Pep is free, with no subscriptions or in-app purchases. It has no sign-up,
analytics, or automatic app cloud sync. Workout and weight data stays on-device
unless you choose to export a backup. See
the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md) for storage, support posts, and deletion details.
